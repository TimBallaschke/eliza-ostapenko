Artworktextarea: Collected during the artist’s daily walks, these images capture everyday street scenes: debris, temporary barriers, objects in decay. These found still lifes, isolated from their original context and presented as staged, almost theatrical compositions, invite the viewer to look closer at what usually goes unnoticed.
