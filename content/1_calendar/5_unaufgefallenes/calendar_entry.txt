Info-textarea: In the fifth edition of the *SameSame* series, Gerald Chors and Elizaveta Ostapenko explore the themes of transience, space and transformation. Both artists work with fragments and deal with the transformation of structures - Chors captures vanished urban scenes in his Polaroids, while Ostapenko breaks up and reassembles everyday orders. Their works move between the poles of documentation and re-creation, past and present.
