Info-textarea: This spring, artists from the cities of Tainan, Salzburg, Hamburg, and Strasbourg are participating in a joint exhibition in Dresden. They will present their artistic works as part of a collaboration between Grafikwerkstatt Dresden and the Office for Culture and Monument Protection of Dresden. The exhibition coincides with the artists’ working residency at the Grafikwerkstatt.
